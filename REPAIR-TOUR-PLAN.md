## STEP 3 — ANTI-GHOST OPERATING SYSTEM (starts the day the Repair Tour ends)

1. **MAX 3 active projects.** School-heavy weeks: MAX 1. You don't take job #4 until one closes. Overcommitment is where the ghosting started.
2. **Quote delivery dates +20% buffer.** If it takes 2 days, promise 3. Early delivery = reputation. Late promise = ghosting fuel.
3. **The 24-Hour Reply Rule.** No client message goes unanswered for 24h. Even "got it, will respond properly tomorrow" counts.
4. **The 5-Minute Update Habit.** Every active project gets ONE status update daily — even if it's just "still on it, on schedule." Silence is what clients experience as disrespect.
5. **The 2-Minute Ghost-Buster Rule.** The moment you feel the urge to avoid a client thread (shame, anxiety, "later"): send a ONE-LINE update within 2 minutes. "I'm behind — new delivery is Thursday, sorry." A bad update ALWAYS beats silence. This one rule kills ghosting.
6. **Friday Review (15 min, weekly).** List every open project + every unanswered client message. Close every gap before the weekend.
7. **Track your GHOST-STREAK: consecutive days with zero dropped client threads.** Write the number down daily. Protect the streak like your income depends on it — because it does.
